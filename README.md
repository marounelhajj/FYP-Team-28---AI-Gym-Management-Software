# AI Gym Management Software

**EECE 501 – Final Year Project | Team 28**
American University of Beirut, Course Coordinator: Dr. Youssef Tawk

## Project Description

This project analyzes existing gym management systems to identify a business
opportunity, then designs and builds a multi-branch AI-powered gym management
platform. The system covers member management, class scheduling, billing,
staff operations, and marketing, and layers AI features on top: a chatbot,
personalized workout/nutrition plans, attendance forecasting, and at-risk
member (churn) detection.

The system is expected to be scalable, resilient, and highly available, and
is deployed on the cloud.

### Key Features
- **Chatbot** – answers member questions (hours, class schedules, membership
  status) without staff involvement.
- **AI-generated personalized plans** – workout and nutrition plans tailored
  to each member's goals, preferences, and constraints.
- **Membership & billing** – sign-up, renewals, freezes/cancellations,
  payment methods, waivers, and loyalty points/rewards.
- **Class & PT scheduling** – booking, waitlists, capacity limits, coach
  availability, substitutions, and attendance forecasting.
- **Branch operations** – live headcount, staffing alerts, inventory, and
  supply reorder requests.
- **Analytics & reporting** – cross-branch financial, retention, attendance,
  and marketing dashboards for general/branch managers.
- **Retention tools** – ranked at-risk member lists with contact reasons for
  proactive outreach.
- **Security & compliance** – role-based access control, two-factor auth for
  staff, audit logging, and member data export/deletion requests.

## User Roles

- **General Manager** – cross-branch financial, retention, and operational
  oversight.
- **Branch Manager** – local scheduling, budget, staffing, and retention
  management.
- **Receptionist** – check-in, registration, POS, and front-desk support.
- **Coach** – schedules, session logging, client communication, and program
  approval.
- **Member** – bookings, chatbot, AI plans, billing, and profile management.
- **Marketing Staff** – segmentation, campaigns, and engagement analytics.
- **System Administrator** – accounts, roles/permissions, and audit logs.
- **Prospective Member** – plan browsing and online sign-up.
